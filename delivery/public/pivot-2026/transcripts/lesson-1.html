Six formats with different jobs

Run Prompt one in the same Work chat. Ask for an inventory that says what was actually read, who owns it, the reporting period and any extraction problems. The company brief supplies the glossary and source-owner register. The risk PDF supplies the Board targets and context. The workbook supplies finance inputs. The customer CSV supplies counts. The service JSON supplies attempts, completions and incidents. The growth presentation contributes a management perspective that you will test.

Source authority

Authority comes from the approved definition and the person responsible for the source, not from whether a file is a spreadsheet or a polished presentation. The CFO owns revenue, variable costs and acquisition spending. Product analytics owns customer counts. The CTO owns transaction counts. A repeated total in a document corroborates the underlying record; it is not another observation to add. If a file cannot be read, name the problem and reattach it rather than quietly dropping that format.

The active-customer distinction

The growth presentation reports fifty-two thousand login-active customers in June. The customer data also records forty thousand transacting customers. This is the planned discrepancy. Login-active includes people who never completed a transaction. The executive card uses monthly transacting customers under the company glossary. Keep both labels. Do not average the totals, and do not call the presentation mathematically false simply because it answers a different question. In the course checkpoint, forty thousand belongs on the transacting-customer card.

Attribution and reporting dates

Customer origin means where the person joined: web or mobile. Each person and their attributed business activity belongs once to that origin even if they later use both apps. Incident surface is a separate dimension. An incident in the mobile app does not establish the experience of every mobile-acquired customer. Also check the two dates. June is the reporting month, while July finalization allows the full thirty-day activation window to mature. Pause and review your inventory. Continue when all six sources are accounted for and the engagement distinction is clear.
