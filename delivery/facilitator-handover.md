# TFIS Pivot 2026 facilitator handover

Prepare and observe the final validation of a free guided course for C-Suite and Board members who build their own dashboard. Aura is a fictional Malaysian consumer payments company with web and mobile distribution. Learners use the ChatGPT desktop app, their own Plus subscription and the six synthetic source files. The planned session is 60 minutes after preflight.

The course remains a teaching draft. Your first task is one complete technical rehearsal, followed by five executive-beginner observations. Release requires the evidence below; the reference dashboard and seven narrated example videos are available for preparation.

## Start here

1. Ask the course owner to open an authorized preview. Review the setup instructions, seven lessons, source download, prompts, checkpoints, dashboard and brief before the rehearsal.
2. Download the Aura source ZIP from the preview and extract it. Keep its six files together. Keep the publisher answer key and observer forms outside that learner folder.
3. Use a fresh Work chat in a learner-equivalent Plus desktop account. Follow the published instructions and prompts; do not start from the completed example.
4. Record the actual build and its failures in the rehearsal form. Correct blocking teaching problems before inviting the five pilot learners.
5. Return the completed evidence bundle to the course owner for revision and release review.

## Observe five executive beginners

Recruit five C-Suite or Board members who are beginners at building dashboards with ChatGPT. Use P01–P05 in notes. Obtain recording permission separately; do not place personal identities or credentials in the observation CSV. Each participant completes preflight in their own supported Plus account.

Follow the observer protocol in the bundle. Let participants use the written prompts and checkpoint feedback. Observe without supplying the answer key. Record every hint or reminder, including its wording and time. Record total time, system waits, repairs, recovery use, rubric scores, extra purchases and evidence locations.

If dashboard generation exceeds its seven-minute allowance, use the lesson's written assisted-recovery instructions. The learner must create an editable Site in their own account, add the requested visible heading and verify the required numbers. The recovery route still needs rehearsal. Exploring the reference alone is a walkthrough.

Classify outcomes as independent, assisted, walkthrough or incomplete. Independent completion requires the learner's own verified outputs without substantive hints or recovery assets. Do not count assisted or walkthrough outcomes toward the independent timing threshold.

Use the complete publisher answer key for Web, May and the remaining scenario checks. Require the learner to explain the source and arithmetic. Rates use summed numerators and denominators; monthly unique customers are not additive across months. Targets apply only to June/All. Acquisition origin stays fixed even when a customer uses both apps.

The scenario holds CAC and activation constant. It does not prove causation, ROI or earnings. Accept a supported hold, capped experiment or conditional increase. Require two verified observations, a counterargument, missing evidence, an accountable role and a review condition.

Score source judgment, numerical verification, dashboard operation, decision quality and responsible publication from 0–2. The build criterion is at least 8/10, with numerical verification at 2 and every other dimension at least 1. Record assistance separately from the score.
