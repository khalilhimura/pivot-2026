# TFIS Pivot 2026 facilitator handover

Prepare and observe the final validation of a free guided course for C-Suite and Board members who build their own dashboard. Aura is a fictional Malaysian consumer payments company with web and mobile distribution. Learners use the ChatGPT desktop app, their own Plus subscription and the six synthetic source files. The planned session is 60 minutes after preflight.

The owner approved release on 15 November 2026 after confirming the Marin audio, screen-reader experience and actual build are good. The completed Plus rehearsal was about 40 minutes without recovery, with an 8/10 self-check. Five executive pilots are scheduled within the next 48 hours, by 17 November 2026 in Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Their outcomes remain pending; the owner authorized publication before those results.

## Start here

1. Open the public course at https://thefutureissolo.com/pivot-2026/. Review setup, seven lessons, source download, prompts, checkpoints, dashboard and brief before facilitating the pilots. Use an owner-authorized admin preview only when reviewing an unpublished revision.
2. Download the Aura source ZIP from the preview and extract it. Keep its six files together. Keep the publisher answer key and observer forms outside that learner folder.
3. Use a fresh Work chat in a learner-equivalent Plus desktop account. Follow the published instructions and prompts; do not start from the completed example.
4. Review the accepted owner rehearsal and record any remaining metadata or reproducible teaching issues. The owner has approved release. Observe the five pilots next and return actual outcomes without filling unobserved fields.
5. Return the completed evidence bundle to the course owner for revision and release review.
