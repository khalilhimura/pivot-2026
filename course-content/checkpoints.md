# Learner checkpoint card

Use the original sources to explain each answer. A copied value without a source or definition does not close the verification checkpoint.

| Lesson | Result to check | Feedback when the result differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Start with the decision | Six original files; acquisition spending versus activation/reliability; CEO decision owner | Reopen the source folder and company brief before asking for a dashboard. |
| 2 — Make the files intelligible | 40,000 June transacting customers; 52,000 login-active customers | These are different definitions. Do not average, add or silently replace them. |
| 3 — Approve the numbers | June/All revenue MYR600,000; activation 38%; success 300,000/310,000 = 96.77% | Reconcile filtered counts and numerators/denominators. Use weighted rates. |
| 4 — Build the executive view | June/Mobile CAC MYR70, activation 35%, margin 30%; returning to All restores MYR600,000 | Trace the filter to the immutable acquisition origin and update every dependent card together. |
| 5 — Challenge the finding and decide | June/All/+20% gives MYR132,000, 2,000 verified and 760 activated | Those are conditional marginal values at unchanged efficiency; they do not establish ROI or causation. |
| 6 — Write the brief and publish | Reviewed Site and Page open for the owner, show dates/scope and intended access | A returned URL or successful save does not itself prove readable owner access. |
| 7 — Defend the decision | Recommendation, checked evidence, counterargument and change condition | Keep the decision with the executive; identify missing information before widening a claim. |

Score source judgment, numerical verification, operation, decision quality and responsible publication from 0–2. Completion requires at least 8/10, with 2 for numerical verification and at least 1 for every other dimension. Record any recovery build as assisted completion.
