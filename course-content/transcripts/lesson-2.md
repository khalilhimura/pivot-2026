## Revenue and commercial definitions

Start with June revenue. The finance workbook records one hundred and eighty thousand ringgit for web-acquired customers and four hundred and twenty thousand for mobile-acquired customers. Together they produce six hundred thousand ringgit. Revenue is Aura's company fee income, not money held in customer wallets or the total value of payments. Variable costs total three hundred and eighty-four thousand ringgit. Revenue less those costs leaves two hundred and sixteen thousand in contribution, or thirty-six percent. Acquisition spending and fixed overhead are excluded, so this margin is not net profit.

## Acquisition and activation

June has ten thousand newly verified customers. Three thousand eight hundred of that acquisition-month cohort complete their first transaction within thirty days. Activation is therefore thirty-eight percent. The acquisition budget spent in June is six hundred and sixty thousand ringgit. Dividing by new verified customers gives a cost of sixty-six ringgit per verified customer. Dividing by activated customers gives about one hundred and seventy-four ringgit per activated customer. These measures use different denominators and must retain their own labels. Monthly transacting customers include older acquisition cohorts and answer another question.

## Weighted transaction success

Now check reliability. Web-origin customers account for ninety-five thousand completions from ninety-six thousand attempts. Mobile-origin customers account for two hundred and five thousand completions from two hundred and fourteen thousand attempts. Add the completions and attempts before dividing. Three hundred thousand divided by three hundred and ten thousand gives ninety-six point seven seven percent. A simple average of the two origin percentages would give the smaller group too much weight. Incident duration in minutes is context; it is not the denominator for this transaction-success measure.
