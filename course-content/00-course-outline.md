# TFIS Pivot 2026 course outline

**Audience:** C-Suite and Board members building their own dashboard; executive beginners, no coding required.

**Title:** From Business Files to Board Decisions: Build Aura's Executive Dashboard in 60 Minutes.

**Core tools:** ChatGPT desktop with Plus, Work, Sites and Pages. Language English; company Aura; Malaysian B2C wallet/payments business; online distribution only; synthetic MYR data.

| Lesson | Duration | Instruction | Learner activity | System time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 Start with the decision | 5 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| 2 Make the files intelligible | 8 | 3 | 4 | 1 |
| 3 Approve the numbers | 10 | 4 | 5 | 1 |
| 4 Build the executive view | 17 | 5 | 5 | 7 |
| 5 Challenge the finding and decide | 10 | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| 6 Write the brief and publish | 8 | 2 | 4 | 2 |
| 7 Defend the decision | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| Total | 60 | 20 | 26 | 14 |

The learner workbook is `workbook.md`; the six prompts are `prompts.json` and `prompts.md`. The detailed plan defines the metric contract, dataset and release gates. The source download is `downloads/aura-course-data-v1.zip` at the workspace root. The reference dashboard and brief are recorded in `production/reference-output-register.json`.

All essential teaching is available as text without video or private Page access. Seven narrated reference clips, captions, transcripts and slide specifications now accompany the workbook. They explain the supplied example and do not show a fresh ChatGPT build. The actual Plus build recording and measured one-hour executive-beginner completion remain unverified; this is a teaching draft.
