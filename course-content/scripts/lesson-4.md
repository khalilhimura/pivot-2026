## Slide 3 What the illustration omits

[SHOW the matching reference slide]

The arithmetic does not know whether acquisition efficiency will remain constant at a higher budget. It does not model retention, lifetime value, fraud, capacity or the cost and benefit of improving the product. It is an incremental monthly illustration used to inform a quarterly discussion, not a forecast of quarterly earnings. The purpose is to expose the assumption so you can ask what evidence would support it. Do not treat a precise-looking number as greater certainty than the input assumptions justify.

## Slide 4 Your recommendation

[SHOW the matching reference slide]

A defensible recommendation might hold the broad increase, approve a capped experiment or stage an increase after defined improvements. Choose the action yourself. Cite two observations, state the strongest counterargument and identify missing evidence. For example, a hold could miss an acquisition opportunity while revenue is growing. A controlled experiment might address that concern. Name a condition that would change your mind, and make its owner explicit. Pause now and prepare those elements before asking ChatGPT to write the decision brief.
