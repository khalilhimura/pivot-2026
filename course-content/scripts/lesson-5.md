Instruction budget: 2 minutes including reading and explanation. Actual rendered duration is recorded in the media manifest. Narration uses a disclosed local synthetic voice.

## Slide 1 The Pages decision brief

Run Prompt five to create your Pages brief. Keep it to about four hundred to five hundred words. It should reflect the recommendation you chose, with the reporting month, customer origin and source version stated explicitly. Include the verified observations, counterargument, missing evidence, scenario assumptions, accountable executive role and review condition.

Read the brief before publishing. Confirm that observed facts are separate from scenario outputs and that an AI proposal has not become an invented Board agreement. The example brief is one supported position, not the required answer. Your own brief remains a fixed decision record when someone explores different dashboard filters.
