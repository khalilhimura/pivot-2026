# Executive beginner pilot protocol

No pilot observations have been collected. Recruit five C-Suite or Board members who are beginners at building dashboards with ChatGPT. Use participant codes P01–P05; do not publish names, emails, credentials or recordings without the participant's permission.

## Before the clock

Confirm each participant has the supported desktop app, Plus, available usage, Sites, Pages, internet and the unmodified six-file synthetic Aura pack. Confirm that the course, prompts and downloads open. Record app/account capability gaps as preflight failures. Do not silently substitute a different subscription or a producer account.

Explain that this evaluates the instructions and product flow, and that the participant owns the business recommendation. Ask the participant to say which evidence they used when accepting a number. Obtain any recording permission separately; observation notes are sufficient when recording is declined.

## During the 60 minutes

Start after preflight. Use the exact course version and prompts recorded on the result sheet. Observe rather than steer. Record lesson start/end times, actual system waits, questions, failures, repairs and any help. A neutral reminder to follow the written next step is still an intervention: record its exact wording and time. Record substantive hints and recovery assets as assistance.

Do not stop the clock for generation, deployment, repairs or discussion of missing definitions. Log an interruption unrelated to the course separately; do not erase the original elapsed time. Record actual time even if it exceeds 60 minutes.

## Check the work

Ask the participant to demonstrate June/All and June/Mobile, explain the 52,000/40,000 distinction and weighted success, operate +20%, open their own Site and Page, and defend their recommendation. Use the course's five 0–2 rubric dimensions. Numerical verification must score 2, every other dimension at least 1, and total at least 8.

Classify the outcome:

- **Independent:** builds and verifies their own outputs without substantive hints or recovery assets.
- **Assisted:** builds with substantive guidance or a prepared compatible build; record the help.
- **Walkthrough:** explores the completed example without building the required outputs.
- **Incomplete:** required outputs or verification remain unfinished.

A pass for the release timing criterion requires independent completion, all rubric gates, at most 60 minutes after preflight and no additional service purchase.

## Release decision

At least four of five must meet the independent timing criterion. All five must understand that definition conflicts need reconciliation, rates need appropriate weighting and scenario assumptions do not prove ROI or causation. Revise confusing instructions and repeat the affected checks. If the timing criterion is missed, simplify the build or revise the advertised duration before launch; do not recategorize assisted completion.

Store observations in `observations.csv`; summarize actual results, sample limitations and revisions in `../pilot-results.md`. Empty cells and pending outcomes mean no observation, never a zero-minute completion or a failed learner.
